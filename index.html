Why water matters

Body functions

Temperature control — Sweat cools your skin when you get too hot.

Waste removal — Kidneys and bowels need water to remove waste as urine and stool.

Joint protection — Water acts as a cushion for your bones and spinal cord.

Cell transport — Blood is mostly water and moves oxygen and nutrients to all parts of the body.

Health benefits

Better brain power — Drinking enough water helps you focus, think clearly, and keep a good mood.

Good digestion — Water breaks down food and stops constipation.

Healthy kidneys — Proper hydration lowers your risk of getting kidney stones.

Physical energy — It keeps your muscles strong and prevents early tiredness during play or exercise.
